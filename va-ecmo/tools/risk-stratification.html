✅ HIGHLY FAVOURABLE - Proceed with VA-ECMO in Bristol

Patient suitable for VA-ECMO initiation on Bristol site.

  • Proceed with cannulation as per protocol
  • Notify RBH/HH ECMO team as per standard pathway
  • Plan for weaning assessment at 5-7 days
  • Consider transfer to advanced heart failure centre only if not weaning by day 5-7

⚠️ INTERMEDIATE RISK - Case-by-case discussion required

Proceed with caution. MDT discussion with RBH/HH ECMO team strongly recommended.

  • Immediate "shock call" with RBH/HH ECMO consultant
  • Consider additional risk factors (SAVE score class III-V)
  • If proceeding: Plan for rapid transfer to RBH/HH if not improving in 48-72 hours
  • Consider bridging strategy to destination therapy

🔴 HIGH RISK - Rapid transfer to advanced heart failure centre

High mortality risk. Should NOT remain on Bristol site.

  • Contact RBH/HH ECMO team immediately via "shock call"
  • If cannulating in Bristol: Plan immediate transfer post-cannulation
  • Consider direct transfer to RBH/HH for cannulation if patient stable enough
  • Ensure SAVE score calculated (Class III-V patients need advanced centre)
  • High likelihood of requiring destination therapy (VAD/transplant)

🔵 INAPPROPRIATE - VA-ECMO generally not suitable

Very high mortality risk with poor outcomes expected.

  • VA-ECMO contraindicated unless exceptional circumstances
  • Mandatory discussion with RBH/HH ECMO consultant before proceeding
  • Consider alternative management strategies
  • If proceeding despite high risk: Immediate transfer to RBH/HH essential
  • Early palliative care involvement recommended

📋 Additional Assessment Required

SAVE Score Calculation: All patients should also have a SAVE score calculated. Patients with SAVE score Class III-V should be discussed with RBH/HH regardless of risk stratification result.
