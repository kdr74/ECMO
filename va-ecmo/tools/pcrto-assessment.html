Pump-Controlled Retrograde Trial Off (PCRTO)

Advanced VA-ECMO Weaning Assessment Protocol

📋 Clinical Indication

PCRTO is performed when conventional weaning to minimum ECMO flows (1 L/min) yields borderline results. This protocol allows fuller cardiovascular loading by creating controlled retrograde flow while preventing circuit thrombosis.

⚠️ Important Safety Note

Patient will be OFF ECMO during this assessment. Ensure full team readiness, emergency equipment available, and clear abort criteria established before proceeding.

PCRTO Protocol Steps

Critical Setup Requirements

  • Sweep gas flow: Switch OFF completely
  • Flow probe: Reverse direction to measure retrograde flow
  • Pump RPM: Reduce to create controlled passive retrograde flow
  • Target retrograde flow: 1.0-1.5 L/min (pump acts as brake)
  • Maximum time at minimal flows: Avoid extended periods (thrombosis risk)

⚠️ Excessive Retrograde Flow Warning

  • If retrograde flow >2.0 L/min: Risk of RV/pulmonary circulation overload
  • Action: Increase pump RPM to reduce retrograde flow
  • Remember: Pump head acts as resistor to prevent excessive passive flow

📊 Weaning Success Criteria with PCRTO

Patient likely weanable if ALL of the following are met:

  • LVEF >20-25%
  • LVOT VTi ≥10 cm
  • MV S' (lateral) ≥6 cm/s
  • Recruitment of LV/RV function (qualitative or quantitative)
  • No significant LV distention
  • No RV failure
  • No cardiac tamponade
  • Stable haemodynamics maintained
