Inclusion Criteria for E-CPR

Refractory cardiac arrest with failure to achieve ROSC after 3 cycles of ALS

Initial rhythm: VF, VT, or PEA (NOT asystole)

🛑 Rapid STOP Criteria

DO NOT PROCEED IF CRITERIA MET

Screen rapidly during ongoing CPR

CLINICAL
Any of the below:

  • Unwitnessed arrest
  • First rhythm asystole
  • No flow time >10 mins (no CPR)
  • Low flow time predicted >60 mins (arrest to ECLS)
  • Terminal illness
  • Severe neurological impairment
  • Non-cardiac/pulmonary cause of arrest
  • Severe co-morbidities

PHYSIOLOGICAL
2 or more of the below:

  • Lactate >18 mmol/L
  • SpO₂ <85% or PaO₂ <6.6 kPa
  • EtCO₂ <1.3 kPa

📋 Activation Workflow:

  1. Continue ALS protocol while gathering clinical information from notes
  2. If NO clinical STOP criteria: Call 2222 → "This is an E-CPR call"
  3. ECMO consultant assesses and decides whether to proceed
  4. If proceeding: Take ABG for physiological criteria
  5. If <2 physiological criteria: Activate full E-CPR team

⚠️ ALS Algorithm Modifications When E-CPR Confirmed

  • STOP adrenaline boluses
  • STOP all defibrillation
  • STOP all other vascular access attempts
  • Use LUCAS device for high-quality compressions
  • Pause compressions only for ECMO needle insertion if absolutely necessary
  • STOP all chest compressions once ECMO flows established
📋 E-CPR Assessment Form

✅ PROCEED WITH E-CPR

Patient meets criteria for E-CPR activation.
Continue CPR while ECMO team mobilizes.
Apply ALS modifications as listed above.

🛑 STOP - Clinical Contraindication

Clinical STOP criteria met.
E-CPR is not appropriate.
Continue standard ALS or consider termination of resuscitation as per guidelines.

🛑 STOP - Poor Physiological Viability

≥2 physiological STOP criteria met.
Predicted poor outcome with E-CPR.
Discuss with ECMO consultant regarding cessation of resuscitation.
