VA-ECMO Clinical Guidelines

University Hospitals Bristol and Weston NHS Foundation Trust

Overview

Veno-Arterial Extracorporeal Membrane Oxygenation (VA-ECMO) is a form of mechanical circulatory support implemented for patients where optimal conventional therapies have failed:

  • Cardiogenic shock with high SCAI grading (SCAI D or E)
  • Refractory cardiac arrest without return of spontaneous circulation (ROSC) - termed "E-CPR"

VA-ECMO initiation is a time-sensitive, complex intervention carrying significant mortality and morbidity risk. Selection of appropriate patients is vital and should be guided by experienced members of the MDT.
