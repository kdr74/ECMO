Pump Controlled Retrograde Trial Off (PCRTO)

Used to further load the cardiovascular system during a weaning study if the results from a conventional wean (to minimum 1L/min ECMO flows) are borderline.

PCRTO Procedure

  1. Switch off sweep gas flow
  2. Reverse the flow probe
  3. Reduce ECMO console revs (initially to 1000 RPM, then titrate)
  4. Achieve retrograde pump flow of 1-1.5 L/min

Key Points:

  • The retrograde flow is passive (as PArt will be greater than PVen)
  • The amount of retrograde flow achieved is determined by how low the RPM is dropped
  • The pump head and forward RPM are acting as a brake to stop excessive retrograde flows
  • Prevents overloading of right heart / pulmonary circulation
  • The retrograde flow is measured by the reversed flow probe
  • Provides fuller loading of cardiovascular system without risk of ECMO circuit clotting

A: Conventional Wean (1 L/min)

  • ↓LV afterload
  • ↑Svit
  • Empty RV
  • ↓venous return
  • ↓LV preload

B: PCRTO (Retrograde Flow)

  • ↓↓LV afterload
  • ↓SVR
  • ↑RV filling
  • ↑venous return
  • ↑↑LV preload

Success Criteria Reminders

At Minimum ECMO Flow or During PCRTO:

  • LVEF >20-25%
  • LVOT VTI ≥10 cm
  • MV S' (lateral) ≥6 cm/s
  • Recruitment of LV and/or RV function (qualitatively or quantitatively)
  • Absence of significant LV dilatation
  • No abnormal decrease of LV dimensions in presence of RV dilation
  • No right ventricular failure
  • No cardiac tamponade

Haemodynamic Stability:

  • MAP not falling below 55 mmHg or >20 mmHg drop
  • CVP or MPAP rise <5 mmHg or remaining <15 mmHg (MPAP <35 mmHg)
  • No significant increase in vasoactives (noradrenaline rise <0.05 mcg/kg/min)
