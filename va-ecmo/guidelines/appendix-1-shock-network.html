Appendix 1: Cardiogenic Shock Network

Regional network structure for cardiogenic shock and VA-ECMO services

Network Structure: The cardiogenic shock service is organised as a regional network with UHBW Bristol functioning as a Cardiogenic Shock Centre, working in close collaboration with the Royal Brompton Hospital (RBH) and Harefield Hospital (HH) as the regional Advanced Heart Failure Centre.

Patient Flow Through Network

Cardiogenic Shock Presentation

  1. Initial Presentation: Patient presents to local hospital or directly to UHBW Bristol
  2. Early Recognition: SCAI classification used to grade severity (target: SCAI D-E for VA-ECMO consideration)
  3. MDT Discussion: Early liaison between local team, Bristol ECMO consultant, and RBH/HH via "shock call" (Microsoft Teams recommended)
  4. Transfer Decision:
    • If suitable for VA-ECMO: Transfer to Bristol for initiation
    • If requires immediate advanced MCS: Consider direct transfer to RBH/HH

Post-ECMO Cannulation at Bristol

  1. Initial Assessment Period: Up to 7 days on Bristol site
  2. Daily MDT Review: Bristol ECMO, cardiology, ICU consultants + liaison with RBH/HH
  3. Destination Decision by Day 5-7:
    • Bridge to Recovery: Wean locally if recovery evident and sustained
    • Bridge to Destination Therapy: Transfer to RBH/HH for VAD/transplant assessment
    • Bridge to Decision: Combined MDT decides continued MCS vs WLST

Post-Cardiotomy Patients

  • Initial care: CICU (C604) for first 48-72hrs
  • After 48-72hrs: Decision regarding:
    • Transfer to A600 (general ICU)
    • Remain on CICU
    • Transfer to RBH/HH
  • Decision involves: Bristol ECMO, CICU, cardiac surgical consultant + RBH/HH teams

Communication and Coordination

Shock Call Protocol

  • Platform: Microsoft Teams (recommended)
  • Participants: Local MDT + Bristol ECMO consultant + RBH/HH ECMO consultant
  • Purpose: Real-time discussion of patient candidacy, risk stratification, and transfer planning
  • When: For all SCAI D-E patients being considered for VA-ECMO, especially those with:
    • High-risk features (Red/Blue on risk matrix, SAVE class III-V)
    • Complex decision-making
    • Post-cardiotomy support

Key Network Principles

  • Early liaison: Contact Bristol ECMO consultant early in patient trajectory
  • Shared decision-making: Bristol and RBH/HH work collaboratively
  • Patient-centred: Right patient, right place, right time
  • Transparent triage: SCAI classification and SAVE score guide decisions
  • Bidirectional transfer: Bristol can initiate ECMO and transfer to RBH/HH; RBH/HH can receive direct referrals

Network Coverage Area

Geographic Scope:

South West England and South Wales - covering approximately 22 hospitals from:

  • West: Cornwall
  • North: Birmingham / Oxford
  • East: Southampton
  • Wales: South Wales region

Service Commissioning Status

Current Status (as of guideline version 1.8):

  • Whilst awaiting commissioning: All patients (excluding post-cardiotomy) will be transferred to RBH/HH once stability achieved
  • Once commissioned: Selected patients will remain on Bristol site for full care including weaning
  • Post-cardiotomy patients: May remain on Bristol site (CICU) for initial 48-72hrs regardless of commissioning status
