Clinical Reference Ranges & Calculation Formulas

Lung-Protective Ventilation Targets:

  • Tidal Volume: 6-8 mL/kg PBW (ARDS: 4-6 mL/kg)
  • Driving Pressure: <15 cmH₂O (optimal), 15-20 (caution), >20 (high risk)
  • Plateau Pressure: <30 cmH₂O (safe), 30-35 (caution), >35 (high risk)
  • Mechanical Power: <12 J/min (safe), 12-17 (caution), >17 (high risk)
  • Total Strain: <1.0 (safe), 1.0-2.0 (caution), >2.0 (high risk)
  • ARDS Severity (P/F ratio in kPa): Mild ≥40, Moderate 26.7-40, Severe <26.7
  • ARDS Severity (P/F ratio in mmHg): Mild ≥300, Moderate 200-300, Severe <200
  • Recruitability (ΔCrs): >1.2 fold change indicates good recruitment potential

Calculation Formulas:

  • Predicted Body Weight (PBW):
    • Male: 50 + 0.91 × (Height in cm - 152.4)
    • Female: 45.5 + 0.91 × (Height in cm - 152.4)
  • Body Mass Index (BMI): Weight (kg) / Height (m)²
  • Tidal Volume per PBW: VT (mL) / PBW (kg)
  • Minute Ventilation (MV): VT (L) × Respiratory Rate (bpm)
  • I:E Ratio: 1 : (Expiratory Time / Inspiratory Time)
    • Expiratory Time = (60 / RR) - Inspiratory Time
  • Driving Pressure (ΔP): Plateau Pressure - PEEP
  • Static Compliance (Crs): VT / Driving Pressure
  • Airway Resistance (Raw): (PIP - Pplat) / Flow (L/s)
  • Mechanical Power: 0.098 × RR × VT × (PIP - 0.5 × ΔP) / 1000
  • P/F Ratio: PaO₂ / FiO₂ (as decimal)
  • Oxygenation Index (OI): (FiO₂ × mPAW) / PaO₂
  • Dead Space (%): ((PaCO₂ - EtCO₂) / PaCO₂) × 100
  • Dead Space Index: EtCO₂ / PaCO₂
  • Theoretical FRC:
    • Male: 2.34 × Height (m) + 0.009 × Age - 1.09
    • Female: 2.24 × Height (m) + 0.001 × Age - 1.00
  • Actual FRC: Compliance (mL/cmH₂O) × 1000 / 40
  • Dynamic Strain: VT / Actual FRC
  • Total Strain: (VT + Compliance × PEEP) / Actual FRC
  • Intrapulmonary Shunt: ((CaO₂ - CvO₂) / (CaO₂ - CvO₂ + 5)) × 100
    • CaO₂ = 1.34 × Hb × (SaO₂/100) + 0.003 × PaO₂
    • Assumes Hb = 15 g/dL
  • Resting Energy Expenditure (REE) - Harris-Benedict:
    • Male: 88.362 + (13.397 × Weight) + (4.799 × Height) - (5.677 × Age)
    • Female: 447.593 + (9.247 × Weight) + (3.098 × Height) - (4.330 × Age)
  • VCO₂: (REE × 0.8) / 7.2
  • ΔVt (%): ((VT_DOWN - VT_UP) / VT_DOWN) × 100
  • ΔCrs (fold change): Compliance_DOWN / Compliance_UP

APRV Mode Parameters:

  • Phigh: Upper pressure level (typically 25-30 cmH₂O)
  • Plow: Lower pressure level (typically 0-10 cmH₂O)
  • Thigh: Duration at Phigh (typically 4-6 seconds)
  • Tlow: Duration at Plow (typically 0.4-0.8 seconds)
  • Expiratory Flow Termination: Percentage of peak expiratory flow at which to terminate release (typically 50-75%)
