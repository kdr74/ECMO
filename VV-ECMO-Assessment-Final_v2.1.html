Age-Stratified Outcomes on VV-ECMO

Fernando SM, Brodie D, Barbaro RP, et al. (2024). Age and associated outcomes among patients receiving venovenous extracorporeal membrane oxygenation for acute respiratory failure: analysis of the Extracorporeal Life Support Organization registry.
Intensive Care Med. 50(3):395-405.
DOI: 10.1007/s00134-024-07343-5
PMID: 38376515

ELSO registry analysis of 27,811 VV-ECMO patients (2017-2022). Demonstrated progressive increase in mortality with each decade of age starting from age 30. Age 70-79: OR 4.57 for hospital mortality. Age ≥80: OR 8.08 compared to reference (18-29 years). Overall hospital mortality 41.5%.
