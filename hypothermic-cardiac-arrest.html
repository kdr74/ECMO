⚠️ Critical Pathway - Time-Sensitive Decision

This pathway differs from standard ECMO criteria. Primary rewarming strategy is cardiopulmonary bypass (CPB) in cardiac theatres.

  • Temperature threshold is age/health dependent: <30°C (young/healthy) OR <32°C (elderly/multimorbid)
  • HOPE survival probability ≥10% is an activation criterion
  • Drowning cases have specific time-based exclusions
  • Tool calculates HOPE survival probability automatically

    🔧 Cardiopulmonary Bypass Rewarming Protocol

    Pre-Theatre Management

    • Continue high-quality CPR (LUCAS device essential)
    • Gentle handling - avoid inducing VF
    • NO rhythm checks until in theatre
    • Intubate and secure airway
    • Establish IV/IO access
    • Send 2× samples for immediate crossmatch: 6 units PRC + 4 units FFP
    • Minimise transfer equipment: ONLY LUCAS, power cable, oxygen cylinder

    Theatre Setup

    • Transfer directly to cardiac theatre
    • Femoral-femoral CPB access (default approach)
    • Continue CPR until on bypass
    • Anticipate profound coagulopathy and pulmonary oedema

    Rewarming Strategy

    • Initial phase: Arterial blood from HLM set to current core temp + 10°C (maximum 37°C)
    • After ROSC or core >32°C: Rewarm at 2-5°C/hour until core >36.5°C
    • VF is common during rewarming - DC cardiovert as needed

    Outcomes After Normothermia (36.5°C)

    • Cardiac activity resumes: Attempt to wean from CPB
      • If haemodynamically stable (±vasoactives): Disconnect from CPB, transfer to ICU
      • If haemodynamically unstable: Call Royal Brompton ECMO service (07815 494 175)
    • No coordinated cardiac activity after achieving 36.5°C: Patient deceased

    Post-Procedure Care

    • Anticipate: glucose dysregulation, pancreatitis, liver injury
    • Log case with ELSO registry (elso.org/registry)
    • Log case with International Hypothermia Registry (hypothermia-registry.org/access) - contact Dr Stefan Gurney for credentials
    • Coroner referral mandated for any hypothermia-related deaths
